❓ FAQ

Common Questions

Is Habesha Hearts free to use? ▼
Yes! Habesha Hearts is free to download and use. Free members can browse profiles, send likes, and post once per week. Premium and Premium+ subscriptions unlock unlimited likes, messaging boosts, profile highlights, and more.
Who is the app for? ▼
Habesha Hearts is designed for Ethiopian and Eritrean singles worldwide — whether you were born there or grew up in the diaspora. If you identify as Habesha and are looking for meaningful connections, this app is for you.
How does the voice bio work? ▼
You can record up to 15 seconds of audio as your voice bio directly from your profile page. Other users can listen to your voice bio before messaging — giving them a sense of your personality before you connect.
Can I pay for Premium in Ethiopian Birr? ▼
Yes! We support payment in both USD and Ethiopian Birr (ETB) via Wegagen Bank and Oromia Bank, in addition to international cards. Pricing is calculated at the current exchange rate (approximately 1 USD = 160 ETB).
Is it available on iPhone? ▼
Currently available on Android via Google Play. The iOS (App Store) version is coming soon. Join the waitlist by downloading the Android version and we'll notify you when iOS launches.
How is safety handled? ▼
We have a full report and block system. You can report any user for inappropriate behavior and our team reviews every report. Profile verification adds an extra layer of trust to the community.

🛡 TRUST & SAFETY

Your Safety is Our Top Priority

Habesha Hearts is built with multiple layers of protection to keep every member safe, respected, and protected from abuse or fraud.

✅
Profile Verification
Users can apply for a blue verified badge. Our team manually reviews verification requests to confirm real identity — no bots, no catfishing.
🔒
Privacy Protection
Your data is stored securely on Firebase with industry-standard encryption. You control what's visible on your profile — location, photos, and bio are all adjustable.
🚨
Report System
Every profile has a Report button. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours by our moderation team. Violations result in immediate account suspension.
🚫
Block System
Block any user instantly. Blocked users cannot see your profile, send messages, or interact with your posts in any way.
🕵️
Anti-Scam Measures
Automated fraud detection monitors suspicious behavior — mass messaging, unusual activity patterns, and financial solicitation are flagged and reviewed immediately.
📋
Community Guidelines
All members agree to our community guidelines: respectful language, no harassment, no explicit content, and genuine intentions. Violations are zero-tolerance.
