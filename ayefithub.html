❓ FAQ

Got Questions?

Is AYE Fit Hub really free? ▼
Yes — 100% free. All programs, PDF guides, and YouTube videos are completely free. No subscription, no hidden fees, no paywall. Ever.
Do I need gym equipment? ▼
No. The Home Workout Series requires zero equipment. For the Strength program, basic equipment (dumbbells, resistance bands) is helpful but optional versions are provided.
How long are the daily sessions? ▼
Most sessions are 20–45 minutes. HIIT sessions are shorter (15–25 min), while strength and core sessions run 30–45 min. Recovery days are light, under 20 minutes.
Where do I find the workouts? ▼
All videos are on YouTube at @ayefithub. PDF guides are downloadable from ayetechub.com/pdfs.html. Join Telegram at t.me/ayetechub for daily reminders and Q&A.
I'm a complete beginner — is this for me? ▼
Absolutely. Day 1 starts from zero. The program builds gradually with clear instructions, modifications, and beginner-friendly explanations for every exercise.
How often are new days released? ▼
New content drops weekly. Days 1–12 are already live. Follow @ayefithub on YouTube and TikTok, or join the Telegram group to get notified when new days go live.
