2. General & support contact

For account help, general questions, feedback, or anything not covered by a specific policy below: ayetechub@gmail.com.

3. Legal & privacy contact

For privacy requests, legal notices, refund requests, appeals, or any formal correspondence: the same address, ayetechub@gmail.com, is actively monitored for legal and privacy matters, not only general support. Please state clearly in your subject line or first line that your message concerns a legal or privacy matter so it's routed correctly.

4. How to serve a legal notice

If you need to formally notify AYE Tech Hub of a legal claim, a copyright or trademark concern, a court order, or any other legal matter, send it to ayetechub@gmail.com with sufficient detail to identify the matter — the nature of the claim, the account or content involved, and your contact information. We do not currently maintain a public physical mailing address for legal service; email is our official channel for legal correspondence at this time.
