🚀 Getting Started

How do I create an account?
Download AYE Market from Google Play, open the app, and sign up with your email, phone number, or Google account. Pick which roles apply to you — Buyer, Seller, Job Seeker, Employer — you can select more than one and change this anytime in Settings.
Is AYE Market free to use?
Yes. Browsing, searching, posting listings, chatting, applying to jobs, and posting job openings are all completely free, with zero commission on anything sold. Optional paid upgrades (7-Day Promotion, Premium, Premium+) only add extra visibility — they're never required.
What roles can one account have?
Buyer, Seller, Job Seeker, and Employer — toggle any combination on or off in Settings. Switching a role off just hides that section of the app; nothing you've posted is ever deleted.
Which languages does the app support?
Tigrinya (ትግርኛ), Amharic (አማርኛ), and English, fully translated across every screen. Change it anytime in Settings → Preferences → Language.
What devices does AYE Market work on?
Android 8.0 (Oreo) and above. An iOS version is planned but not yet available.

🛍️ Buying

How do I search for something specific?
Use the search bar for keywords (it matches singular and plural forms automatically), then narrow results with category filters — price range, condition, brand, and category-specific attributes like bedrooms or mileage.
How does "nearby" search work?
With location permission enabled, listings can be sorted by actual distance from where you are, using Tigray's real region → zone → wereda → tabiya hierarchy plus GPS.
How do I save a listing or a search?
Tap the heart icon on any listing to add it to Favorites. You can also save a search's filters and get notified when new matching listings appear.
How do I contact a seller?
Every listing shows the contact methods that seller has enabled — in-app Chat, Call, WhatsApp, or SMS. Pick whichever you prefer; the seller decides which channels to accept.
Can I trust the photos on a listing?
Listings should carry real photos of the actual item — this is required by our Listing Policy. If you spot stock or misleading photos, report the listing.

📤 Selling

How do I post a listing?
Tap the post button, choose a category, add up to 8 photos (drag to reorder — the first becomes your cover photo), fill in the category-specific fields, set your price and location, and publish. Your draft auto-saves as you go.
Does my listing need admin approval before it's visible?
No — listings publish immediately and are reviewed reactively, not gated behind pre-approval. Our moderation team can still remove or hide a listing afterward if it's reported or found to violate our policies.
How do I manage my listings?
Go to My Listings to edit, pause, mark as sold, archive, duplicate, or delete any listing at any time.
What does the Seller Dashboard show?
Total views, favorites, and sold counts for your listings. Premium and Premium+ sellers see deeper analytics and trends.
Can I hide my phone number?
Yes. Set your contact preference to chat-only if you don't want to share your number — buyers can still reach you in-app without ever seeing it.
Can I open a business/storefront account?
Yes — add a store name and business presence to your profile so buyers can browse everything you're selling in one place.

📋 Listings

Why was my listing removed?
Usually because it was reported and found to violate our Listing Policy, Prohibited Items Policy, or Community Guidelines — e.g. wrong category, misleading price, duplicate posting, or stock photos. You can contest the decision through our Appeals Process.
Can I post the same item in multiple categories?
No — duplicate or cross-posted listings for the same item are against policy. Pick the single most accurate category.
How many photos can I add?
Up to 8 photos per listing, and you can drag to reorder them — whichever is first becomes the cover photo shown in search results.
What happens to a listing when I mark it Sold?
It's removed from active search results but stays in your My Listings history. Nothing is deleted unless you delete it yourself.

💬 Messaging

Does chat reveal my phone number?
No, never. In-app chat keeps your number private. Call, WhatsApp, and SMS buttons only appear when a seller has separately chosen to share their own number.
Someone is messaging me inappropriately — what do I do?
Report the conversation or the user immediately (see our 8 report reasons, including Harassment and Spam), and use Block to stop them from messaging you again. See our Messaging Policy and Block User Policy.
Are my chat messages kept forever?
Chats are retained for safety and moderation purposes. If you delete your account, your side of past conversations is anonymized rather than fully erased, so the other person's conversation history stays intact. See our Data Retention Policy.

💳 Payments & Premium

What are the paid plans?
7-Day Listing Promotion (200 ETB, boosts one listing for a week), Premium (400 ETB/month, broader visibility and analytics), and Premium+ (600 ETB/month, the highest visibility tier). Everything else in the app is free.
How do I pay?
Payments are currently processed manually: choose your plan, follow the in-app payment instructions, submit your reference number and receipt, and our team reviews and activates it — usually within one business day. Automatic Telebirr and Chapa payments are in development.
What happens when my Premium plan expires?
Your listings are never deleted — they simply return to normal, non-boosted ranking. Renew anytime to restore the boost.
Can I get a refund?
Paid upgrades are generally non-refundable once activated, since the benefit (visibility) is delivered immediately. Exceptions exist for billing errors or platform-side activation failures — see our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
Does AYE Market ever ask for my card or bank PIN?
Never. AYE Market does not collect card numbers, CVVs, or bank PINs anywhere in the app. Any message asking for these, claiming to be AYE Market, is a scam — report it immediately.

✅ Verification

Do I need to verify my identity to use the app?
No — browsing, messaging, buying, selling, and applying to jobs all work without any ID verification. It's entirely optional and adds a trust badge plus CV-viewing rights for employers.
What documents can I use to verify?
A Fayda ID, Kebele Residence ID, Driver's License, Business License, or Passport — reviewed by a real person on our team, never auto-approved.
Who sees my ID documents?
Only our verification team. Never other users, never third parties. See our Identity Verification Policy.
My verification was rejected — what now?
Check that your document is legible, current, and the name matches your account, then resubmit. You can also appeal via our Appeals Process.

🛡️ Reporting & Safety

How do I report a listing or a seller?
Tap Report on any listing or profile and choose a reason: Scam/Fraud, Fake Listings, Spam, Harassment, Offensive Content, Selling Prohibited Items, Impersonation, or Other. Reports go to a human moderation queue.
What's the difference between Block and Report?
Report flags a listing or user to our moderation team for review and possible enforcement. Block is a personal filter — it hides that user's listings from you and stops them from messaging you, without notifying them.
Will I be told what happened to my report?
To protect reporter privacy, we don't share the specific outcome of every report, but every report is reviewed and acted on where warranted.
How do I avoid scams?
Never pay before inspecting an item, meet in public places, and be wary of anyone claiming to be "AYE Market escrow" — we never handle buyer-seller payments. See our full Safety Center.

💼 Jobs

How do I apply for a job?
Build your Job Seeker Profile with your CV, skills, and experience once, then apply to any opening in one tap. Track every application's status — pending, viewed, shortlisted — from your applications dashboard.
Who can see my CV?
Only identity-verified employers. Unverified accounts can see that a CV exists, never its contents.
How do I post a job opening?
As an Employer, use Post a Job with your company name, vacancies, responsibilities, required skills, salary, deadline, and location. Manage postings and applicants from your Employer Dashboard.
What's a "Jobs Wanted" ad?
A candidate-side ad where job seekers advertise their availability, skills, and experience so employers can find and contact them directly — the mirror image of a job posting.

👤 Account Management

How do I change my language, theme, or notification settings?
All in Settings → Preferences. Changes apply instantly, no restart needed.
How do I see who I've blocked?
Settings → Blocked Users lists everyone you've blocked, with an option to unblock at any time.
How do I delete my account?
Visit our Account Deletion page, sign in to verify it's you, and submit your request. Once approved, your profile, listings, photos, CV, and personal data are permanently deleted.
Can I get a copy of my data first?
Yes — request an export anytime via our Privacy Rights page before deleting your account, or independently of deletion.

🔧 Troubleshooting

The app won't let me sign in — what should I check?
Confirm you're using the same sign-in method you registered with (email, phone, or Google), check your internet connection, and make sure the app is updated to the latest version from Google Play.
I'm not receiving notifications.
Check Settings → Notifications inside the app, and make sure notification permission is granted for AYE Market in your phone's system settings.
A photo or CV won't upload.
Check your internet connection and that the file isn't unusually large; try again on Wi-Fi. If it keeps failing, contact support with a screenshot of any error message.
I found a bug or have a feature idea.
Use Settings → Feedback in the app — it creates a ticket our team answers directly, or email ayetechub@gmail.com.
My question isn't answered here.
Email ayetechub@gmail.com or use in-app Settings → Feedback — a real person on our team replies, usually within 1–2 business days.
