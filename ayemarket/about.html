About Us

Building a marketplace Tigray can actually trust

AYE Marketplace is developed by AYE Tech Hub — an Ethiopian engineering and technology studio building practical software for real, local problems.

Our story

AYE Marketplace started from a simple observation: people in Tigray were already buying, selling, and hiring locally every day — through Telegram groups, word of mouth, and physical noticeboards — but had no dedicated, safe, purpose-built app for it. Existing marketplace apps either weren't localized, didn't support local languages well, or weren't built with the specific safety and trust concerns of this market in mind.

So AYE Tech Hub set out to build one from scratch: a marketplace and jobs platform designed specifically around how people in Tigray communicate (chat, call, WhatsApp, SMS — whichever they prefer), where they are (an interactive local map, not just a generic address field), and what they need (buying and selling goods, and finding or posting local jobs, in one place).

Why we focus on Tigray

Rather than trying to be a generic marketplace for all of Ethiopia from day one, AYE Marketplace deliberately started narrow and local. Building for one region first means we can get the details right — local categories, local language support in Tigrinya, an interactive map of Tigray's own regions and cities, and safety guidance that reflects real local conditions — before expanding further.

Core values

  • Safety first. Every feature — verification, moderation, reporting, blocking — exists because trust is the actual product, not a checkbox.
  • Built locally, for local life. Language, location, and communication preferences are designed around how people here actually operate, not adapted from a foreign template.
  • Free to access. Browsing, messaging, and buying are, and will remain, free — premium features add extra visibility, they don't gate basic use.
  • Honest by default. No fake urgency, no inflated numbers, no dark patterns — we'd rather earn trust slowly than lose it fast.

Meet the founder

Awet G. Nway is a mechanical, digital, and automation engineer and the founder of AYE Tech Hub. AYE Marketplace is one of two divisions under AYE Tech Hub — alongside AYE TechEngine & AYE TechPro, the company's engineering-education platform. Awet leads product and engineering for AYE Marketplace end-to-end, from the mobile app to the backend systems that power it.

Our technology

AYE Marketplace is built with Flutter for a fast, native-feeling experience on Android, backed by Firebase for authentication, real-time data, cloud storage, and push notifications. Search, filtering, and location-based discovery run on Cloud Firestore with purpose-built indexes; moderation, notifications, and subscription logic run on Cloud Functions. The app is engineered as a clean, modular codebase organized by feature, so new capabilities — like the Jobs Marketplace or Premium plans — can be added without destabilizing the rest of the app.

What's next

Our roadmap is shaped directly by user feedback once AYE Marketplace launches. Areas we're actively exploring:

  • Automated Telebirr and Chapa payments for Premium plans and listing promotions (currently processed manually by our team — see Pricing).
  • Expansion beyond Tigray into additional Ethiopian regions, once the core experience is proven.
  • Deeper seller analytics and business tools for Premium+ sellers.
  • Continued investment in safety tooling based on real reports from the community.
