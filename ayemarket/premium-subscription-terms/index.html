Premium Subscription Terms

These Premium Subscription Terms describe the optional paid plans available on AYE Market — the 7-Day Listing Promotion, Premium, and Premium+ — and govern how you purchase, use, renew, and lose access to them. They supplement our general Terms of Service and our Payment Policy, which describes exactly how you pay for these plans.

1. The free tier stays free

Nothing in these Terms changes the fact that core use of AYE Market — browsing, posting listings, chatting with buyers or sellers, applying to jobs, and hiring candidates — carries no fee and no sales commission, now or in the future for the same feature set. The plans below are entirely optional visibility and tooling upgrades, not a paywall on using the marketplace.

3. How you subscribe

Every plan is purchased through the manual payment flow described in our Payment Policy: you transfer the amount yourself, submit a payment reference and receipt in the app, and an administrator reviews and approves the request before the plan activates. There is currently no automated in-app checkout.

4. Duration & no auto-renewal

The Listing Promotion runs for a fixed 7 days from activation. Premium and Premium+ run for one calendar month from activation. None of these plans auto-renew or auto-charge you. Because payment is manual and requires you to actively transfer funds and submit a new request each time, there is no mechanism in the app that could charge you again without your direct action. If you want continued benefits, you choose to submit a new payment request before or after the current period ends.

5. What happens at expiry

When a paid period ends without a new approved request:

  • The associated benefits (boosted placement, priority ranking, analytics access) end.
  • Your listings are not deleted, hidden, or penalized — they simply return to standard, non-boosted ranking alongside every other free listing.
  • Any seller tools or badges tied specifically to the plan become unavailable until you renew.
  • You keep full ordinary use of the Service — expiry only removes the paid extras, never your basic access.

6. Renewing

You can submit a new payment request to renew or repurchase a plan at any time, whether or not your previous period has expired. There is no lock-out period and no penalty for letting a plan lapse before renewing.

7. Switching between plans

You may purchase a different tier at any time (for example, moving from Premium to Premium+). Where an existing paid period is still active when a new, higher-tier request is approved, the new plan's benefits take over; we do not currently offer prorated credit for the unused portion of a lower tier when you upgrade mid-period — plan your purchase timing accordingly, or contact us if you believe your situation needs individual review.

8. Price changes

We may change the price of any plan as the Service evolves. Price changes apply only to purchases made after the change takes effect — we will never retroactively charge you more, or reduce the benefits you're owed, for a period you already paid for at the old price.

9. Fair use & misuse

Paid tools are provided for genuine buying, selling, and hiring activity. Using Premium or Premium+ features to post prohibited content, spam, duplicate listings, or otherwise violate our Terms of Service or Community Guidelines can result in the underlying content being removed and the account being suspended per our Account Suspension & Termination Policy, in addition to — not instead of — any refund rules below.

10. Cancellation & refunds

Because manual payment requires your own active transfer, there is no "cancel a subscription" toggle to stop a future charge — nothing charges automatically in the first place. If you no longer want a plan, simply don't submit a renewal. For situations involving an error, a duplicate approval, or a feature that failed to activate through no fault of yours, see our full Refund & Cancellation Policy.

11. Governing law

12. Changes to these Terms

We may update these Terms as our plans evolve, including when automated payment is introduced. Material changes are reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and never apply retroactively to a period you've already paid for.
