v1.0 — Initial Release

Launching soon on Google Play

The first public release of AYE Marketplace, including:

  • Buy & sell listings across 8+ categories with photos, filters, and GPS/map-based search
  • Real-time chat, plus Call, WhatsApp, and SMS contact options
  • Jobs Marketplace — job postings, Jobs Wanted candidate ads, and application tracking
  • Optional phone and ID verification with a visible trust badge
  • Favorites, saved searches, and push notifications
  • Free, Premium, and Premium+ plans, plus 7-day listing promotion
  • Tigrinya, Amharic, and English language support
  • Light and dark mode
