Community Guidelines

What's allowed, what isn't, and how we keep AYE Marketplace trustworthy.

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Allowed listings

You can list anything you legally own and have the right to sell — vehicles, property, electronics, fashion, home goods, and services — as long as your listing is accurate, honest, and belongs in the category you chose. Job postings and Jobs Wanted ads must describe a genuine opportunity or genuine availability for work.

Prohibited listings

The following are never allowed on AYE Marketplace, in any category:

  • Weapons — firearms, ammunition, explosives, or weapon accessories.
  • Illegal products — drugs and controlled substances, stolen goods, and anything illegal to sell under Ethiopian law.
  • Counterfeit items — fake or replica branded goods misrepresented as genuine.
  • Adult content — sexual content, escort/companionship services, or anything sexually exploitative.
  • Live animals in violation of local animal welfare regulations, and endangered species products.
  • Financial schemes — pyramid/Ponzi schemes, fake investment opportunities, or "get rich quick" offers.
  • Personal data for sale — ID documents, account credentials, or other people's private information.

Spam & duplicate listings

Post each item once, in the category it actually belongs to. Posting the same item repeatedly, across multiple categories, or flooding the marketplace with near-identical listings is not allowed and may result in removal of the listings and a warning or suspension.

Fraud & scams

Any attempt to deceive another user — fake listings, bait-and-switch pricing, fake payment confirmations, phishing links, or impersonating another person or business — results in immediate listing removal and likely account suspension. Report anything that looks like a scam; see our Safety Center for how to recognize one.

Harassment

Threats, hate speech, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, or targeted abuse toward any user — inside listings, messages, reviews, or reports — is never tolerated and results in account action, up to permanent suspension.

Copyright & intellectual property

Only use photos and content you own or have permission to use. Don't copy another seller's listing text or photos. If you believe your copyrighted content was used without permission, contact us with details and we will investigate.

Reporting

Every listing, chat, and profile has a Report option. When you report something, tell us specifically what's wrong — this helps our moderation team act quickly and accurately. False or malicious reports made in bad faith may themselves result in account action.

Our moderation process

  1. Automated checks flag listings that match known scam patterns or prohibited-content signals.
  2. User reports route directly to our moderation team's review queue.
  3. Human review — an admin reviews the reported listing/user against these Guidelines.
  4. Action — depending on severity: the listing is removed, the user receives a warning, or the account is suspended or permanently banned.
  5. Appeals — if you believe a moderation decision was made in error, contact us and we'll review it.

Consequences
