Advertising Policy

AYE Market sells no ads. Here's what "promotion" actually means on the platform.

Last updated: July 17, 2026 · Version 1.0

This Advertising Policy explains what AYE Market does not do, and describes the paid promotion tools sellers can use to give their own listings more visibility. It works alongside our Listing Policy and Terms of Service.

1. What AYE Market does not do

AYE Market runs no third-party advertising. We do not sell ad space to outside businesses, we do not display banner ads or sponsored content from companies unrelated to the marketplace, and we do not track your activity across the app or the web to build an advertising profile for targeting purposes. Everything you see promoted in the app is another AYE Market seller's own listing, boosted through the tools below — never a paid placement from an external advertiser.

3. Rules for promoted content

Paying for promotion does not exempt a listing from any rule that applies to ordinary listings. A boosted or Premium listing must still fully comply with our Listing Policy and Community Guidelines — accurate category, honest price, real photos, truthful description. A listing found to violate those policies can be removed and its promotion refunded or forfeited under the same terms as any other removal, regardless of how much was paid to boost it.

4. No implied endorsement

A Premium badge, Premium+ badge, or boosted placement reflects that a seller paid for extra visibility and ranking — it is not a review, verification of quality, or endorsement of the seller or their item by AYE Tech Hub. Sellers may not describe their promoted status as "AYE Market approved," "AYE Market verified" (verification is a separate, unrelated identity process — see our Seller Policy), or otherwise imply an endorsement that promotion does not provide.

5. Manual payment & admin approval

Paid promotion is currently activated through a manual process: you submit a payment reference and receipt in the app, and an administrator reviews and approves the request before boosted placement, badges, or ranking benefits go live. Because approval requires human review, activation isn't instant — see our Terms of Service for the full payments and refund terms that apply to these purchases. Automated payment methods are planned; when introduced, this policy applies equally to them.

6. Enforcement

Misusing promotion — for example, boosting a listing that violates the Listing Policy, or falsely implying AYE Market endorsement — is handled under the same enforcement ladder as any other policy violation: correction or removal for a first minor issue, escalating to suspension for repeated or serious misuse. See the ladder in our Community Guidelines.

7. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia, and forms part of the agreement described in our Terms of Service.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as promotion tools and pricing evolve. Material changes are reflected by updating the "Last updated" date and version above; pricing changes apply only to future purchases, never to a promotion period already paid for.
