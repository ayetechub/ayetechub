Cookie Policy

Short version: this website remembers your theme preference. That's it.

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Scope

This policy covers the AYE Market pages of ayetechub.com. It does not apply to the AYE Market mobile app, which does not use browser cookies at all.

What are cookies and local storage?

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device between visits. We use the closely related localStorage browser feature for the same purpose.

What we actually use

TypePurposeUsed on this site?
Essential (localStorage)Remembering your dark/light theme choiceYes — one key, ayemarket-theme
Analytics / performanceTraffic measurementNo — none installed. If we ever add one, this page updates first
Advertising / trackingAd targeting, retargetingNo — never

Firebase on privacy pages

Our Account Deletion and Privacy Rights pages load the Firebase Authentication SDK so you can sign in to verify your identity. Firebase may use its own browser storage to keep you signed in during that process — governed by Google's Privacy Policy. This happens only on those two pages, only when you use them.

Managing cookies

Your browser's settings let you block or clear cookies and local storage at any time. The only effect here: your theme choice resets to default on your next visit. Everything else on the site works identically.
