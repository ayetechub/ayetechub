Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AYE Tech Hub ("AYE Tech Hub," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the AYE Marketplace mobile application (the "App") and related services (together, the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the Service.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account information: full name, username, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, profile photo, cover photo, and bio, if you choose to add them.
  • Listing content: titles, descriptions, prices, categories, photos, and location details for anything you post.
  • Messages: the content of chats you send through the App.
  • Verification documents: a government-issued ID (Fayda ID, Kebele Residence ID, Driver's License, Business License, or Passport) if you choose to request the verified badge.
  • Job Seeker / CV information: if you use the Jobs Marketplace, your CV/resume file, skills, experience, education, and related career information.
  • Payment information: for Premium plans and listing promotions, we collect the plan selected, amount, a payment reference number, and a payment receipt you upload. We do not collect or store your bank account or card numbers — manual payments are verified by our team from the receipt you provide.
  • Support communications: anything you send us via in-app Feedback & Support, this website's contact form, or email.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Location data: if you grant permission, we collect precise GPS location to power nearby search and distance sorting. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings — the App still works using manually selected region/city.
  • Device information: device model, operating system and version, unique device/installation identifiers, and app version.
  • Usage information: listings you view, searches you perform, favorites, and general app interaction, used to power features like "recently viewed" and your seller dashboard.
  • IP address: collected as part of normal network communication with our backend (Firebase) and by our website host when you visit ayetechub.com.
  • Push notification tokens: a device token used to deliver notifications to you, managed by Firebase Cloud Messaging.

1.3 Cookies and similar technologies (website only)

The AYE Marketplace mobile app does not use browser cookies. This website (ayetechub.com) may use essential cookies/local storage for basic functionality like remembering your dark/light mode preference. See our Cookie Policy for details.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account, and let you sign in.
  • To let you post, browse, and search listings and job posts.
  • To enable messaging between buyers and sellers.
  • To show you nearby listings and power the interactive map.
  • To send you notifications about messages, listing activity, and account updates.
  • To review verification and manual payment submissions.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, scams, and violations of our Community Guidelines.
  • To respond to support requests and improve the Service based on how it's actually used.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

For full technical detail, see our Developer Information page.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in these situations:

  • With other users, as intended: your listing content, public profile info, and messages you send are visible to the people you're transacting with.
  • With verified employers: your CV/resume is only visible to employers whose accounts our team has verified.
  • With service providers: Google/Firebase, as described above, strictly to operate the Service.
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of AYE Tech Hub, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers: if AYE Tech Hub is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

5. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, plus a reasonable period afterward for legal, security, and fraud-prevention purposes. Specific retention notes:

  • Verification documents and payment receipts are retained only as long as needed for review and audit purposes, then deleted on a routine basis.
  • If you delete a listing, it's removed from public view immediately; residual copies may persist briefly in backups before automatic purging.
  • If you delete your account (see Account Deletion), your personal data is permanently deleted, with narrow exceptions described there (e.g. anonymized message content in shared chats, so we don't erase the other participant's own conversation history).

6. How we protect your data

Data in transit between the App and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access to our backend is governed by security rules that restrict who can read or write which data — for example, only you can read your own private messages, and only our verification team can access submitted ID documents. Admin actions are logged for accountability. No system is 100% secure, but we design deliberately around least-privilege access rather than trusting the app alone.

7. Your rights & choices

  • Access & correction: most of your profile information can be edited directly in the app (Settings → Profile).
  • Data export: request a copy of your data via our Data & Privacy Center.
  • Deletion: request permanent account deletion via our Account Deletion page.
  • Location permission: can be revoked anytime in your device's app permission settings.
  • Notifications: can be turned off in Settings → Notifications, or at the OS level.
  • Marketing: we do not currently send marketing emails; if that changes, you'll be able to opt out.

8. Requesting deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your account and associated data at any time through our Account Deletion Request page — sign in to verify it's really you, then submit your request. An administrator reviews and processes every request; see that page for exactly what is and isn't deleted.

9. Children's privacy

AYE Marketplace is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we've collected data from a child under 18, we will delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

10. International users & GDPR readiness

AYE Marketplace is built for users in Ethiopia, and our infrastructure (Firebase/Google Cloud) may process data in regions outside Ethiopia as part of Google's global infrastructure. If you access the Service from the European Economic Area, UK, or other regions with their own data protection laws, we aim to honor the same rights those laws describe — access, correction, deletion, and data portability — through the mechanisms described in this policy, even where not strictly legally required for our current user base.

11. Legal compliance

We comply with applicable Ethiopian law, including data protection and consumer protection requirements, and will disclose information where legally required to do so by a competent authority.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above, and where appropriate, an in-app notice.

13. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data: ayetechub@gmail.com, or use our Contact page.
