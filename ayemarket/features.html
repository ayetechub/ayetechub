Features

Everything you need to buy, sell, and hire — in one app

AYE Marketplace isn't just a listings feed. Here's everything under the hood.

Buying & Selling

🛍️

Buy anything, locally

Browse thousands of categories — vehicles, property, electronics, fashion, and more — all posted by people near you.

📤

Sell in minutes

Post a listing with photos, a price, and your location — no complicated setup, publish and go live immediately.

📸

Multiple photos & zoom gallery

Add up to 8 photos per listing, with a full-screen pinch-to-zoom gallery so buyers can inspect every detail before reaching out.

Search & Discovery

🔍

Powerful search

Keyword search across every category, with instant results as you type.

🎛️

Advanced filters

Narrow results by price range, condition, brand, category-specific attributes, and more — find exactly what you're after.

📍

GPS location search

See listings sorted by distance from where you actually are.

🗺️

Interactive Tigray map

Browse listings visually on an interactive map of Tigray's regions, zones, and cities.

❤️

Favorite listings

Save any listing to your Favorites and come back to it anytime.

🔖

Saved searches

Save a search and get notified when new matching listings are posted.

Messaging & Notifications

💬

Real-time chat

Message any seller directly inside the app — no need to share your phone number if you'd rather not.

📞

Phone call

Call a seller directly when they've made their number available.

🟢

WhatsApp

Jump straight into a WhatsApp conversation about a listing, pre-filled with the item you're asking about.

✉️

SMS

Text a seller directly if that's easier for you.

🔔

Push notifications

Get notified instantly for new messages, price changes on saved listings, application updates, and more.

Trust & Safety

✅

Verified sellers

Sellers can verify their identity with a government ID (Fayda, Kebele ID, passport, license) for a visible trust badge.

🛡️

Admin moderation

Listings are reviewed against our Community Guidelines, and every listing/user can be reported in one tap.

🚫

Block & report

Block anyone who makes you uncomfortable, and report suspicious listings straight to our moderation team.

Selling Power-Ups

⭐

Premium listings

Promote a listing for more visibility and a higher spot in search — see Pricing for details.

🏢

Business accounts

Sellers running a real business can set up a dedicated storefront presence with a store name and profile.

📊

Seller dashboard

Track views, favorites, and listing performance — Premium sellers unlock deeper analytics.

Jobs Marketplace

💼

Post & find jobs

Employers post openings; job seekers browse, filter by category and experience level, and apply with a CV.

🧑‍💻

Jobs Wanted ads

Candidates can post their own "available for hire" ad — employers browse and reach out directly.

📋

Application tracking

Track every application you've sent or received, in one place.

Made to Fit You

🌐

Multi-language

Fully available in Tigrinya, Amharic, and English.

🌓

Light & dark mode

Browse comfortably any time of day — light mode by default, switchable anytime in Settings.

📶

Keeps working on a weak connection

Recently viewed listings stay available even when your connection drops, so a spotty network doesn't stop you browsing.
