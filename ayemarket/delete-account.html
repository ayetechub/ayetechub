Data & Privacy

Delete Your Account

Permanently delete your AYE Marketplace account and personal data. This cannot be undone.

⚠ Before you continue

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed once approved.
  • Your listings, messages, favorites, and profile will be permanently removed.
  • Any active Premium subscription or pending payment request will be cancelled.
  • Messages you sent in shared conversations are anonymized (not deleted outright) so the other participant's chat history isn't broken — your name and account link are removed from them.
  • Want a copy of your data first? Visit the Data & Privacy Center before requesting deletion.

Sign in to verify it's you

We require sign-in so no one can request deletion of an account that isn't theirs.

— or —

Signed up with a phone number only? Email ayetechub@gmail.com from the address on your account, or reach us via in-app Feedback & Support, and we'll verify you manually.

⏳

Your deletion request is pending review

Our team will review it shortly — you'll receive a notification once it's processed. No further action needed.

What happens after you submit

  1. Your request is sent to our admin team's review queue.
  2. An administrator verifies the request (typically within a few business days).
  3. On approval, a server-side process automatically and permanently deletes: your login credentials, your profile, your listings, your favorites, your verification documents, your CV, your subscription/payment records, and your uploaded photos.
  4. Your participation is removed from any shared chat conversations, and your sent messages are anonymized so the other participant's conversation isn't broken.
  5. You'll receive a final confirmation notification before your account access ends.

Questions about deletion? See our Help Center or contact us directly.
