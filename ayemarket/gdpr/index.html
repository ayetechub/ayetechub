AYE Market serves users in Ethiopia, and these tools exist for everyone — not only where a law such as the EU's GDPR mandates them. If you access the Service from a region with its own data protection law (GDPR, UK GDPR, or similar), the rights above map directly onto those laws' requirements, and we honor them the same way.

How requests are handled

  1. Sign in below — proving control of the account is the identity verification; nobody can request your data by just knowing your email address.
  2. Submit — your request is recorded with a pending status.
  3. Human review & fulfilment — our team processes it: exports are compiled and sent to your account email; corrections are applied after review. Typical turnaround is within 7 days, and always within 30.
  4. Everything is logged — privacy requests and the actions taken on them are auditable.

Privacy FAQs

Who can see my phone number? Only people you allow — chat never reveals it; call/WhatsApp/SMS buttons appear only where you've shared a number and enabled those channels.

Who can see my CV? Only identity-verified employers. Unverified accounts see that a CV exists, never its contents.

Who sees my ID verification documents? Only our verification team. Never other users, never third parties.

Do you sell my data? No. No advertising partners, no data brokers, no exceptions. See Data Safety.

Something else? ayetechub@gmail.com — privacy questions answered within 1–2 business days.
