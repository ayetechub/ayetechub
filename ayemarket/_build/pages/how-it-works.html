How It Works

From download to your first sale

Here's exactly what happens at every step — for sellers and buyers.

Selling on AYE Marketplace

Create your account

Sign up with your email, phone number, or Google account in under a minute.

Verify your phone (optional but recommended)

Verifying your number adds a visible badge to your profile and builds buyer trust — but it's optional, you can start selling without it.

Create your listing

Choose a category, write a title and description, and set your price.

Upload photos

Add up to 8 clear photos — listings with real photos get far more responses than those without.

Select your location

Pin your listing's location on the map so nearby buyers can find it.

Publish

Your listing goes live immediately and appears in search and category browsing.

Receive inquiries

Buyers reach out by chat, call, WhatsApp, or SMS — whichever you've made available.

Sell safely

Meet in a public place, confirm the item matches the listing, and mark it Sold once it's done. See our Safety Center for the full guide.

Buying on AYE Marketplace

Browse or search

Explore by category, search by keyword, or filter by price, location, and condition.

Check the listing carefully

Review all photos, the full description, the seller's verification badge, and their rating.

Message the seller

Ask questions before agreeing to anything — availability, condition, final price.

Agree on a safe meeting

Choose a public, well-lit location and bring someone with you if possible.

Inspect before you pay

Confirm the item matches the listing before any money changes hands.

🛡️

Our top safety recommendation

Always keep the conversation and payment arrangement inside the platform where possible, meet in public, and never send money for an item you haven't seen in person (or verified thoroughly for remote deals). Read the full guide in our Safety Center.
