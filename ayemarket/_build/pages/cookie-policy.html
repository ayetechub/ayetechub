Last updated: July 17, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how ayetechub.com (including this AYE Marketplace section) uses cookies and similar local-storage technologies. It does not apply to the AYE Marketplace mobile app, which does not use browser cookies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device to remember information between visits. We also use similar browser storage (like localStorage) for the same purpose.

EssentialRemembering your light/dark mode preferenceYes

MarketingAd targeting or retargetingNo — we don't run advertising cookies

Third-party cookies

If our contact form is connected through a third-party provider (e.g. Formspree), submitting that form may involve that provider's own cookies/processing, governed by their own privacy policy. We link to any such provider directly where used.

Managing cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking essential cookies may mean preferences like dark mode reset each visit — the site will otherwise continue to function normally. You can also clear your browser's local storage manually to reset any saved preference immediately.

We'll update this page if what we use changes. Questions? Contact us.
