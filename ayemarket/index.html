Our mission

Make local commerce work for everyone in Tigray

Too many people still rely on word-of-mouth, physical noticeboards, or unsafe informal channels to buy, sell, or find work locally. AYE Marketplace exists to replace that with something modern, trustworthy, and built specifically for how people here actually communicate and transact.

Our vision

A safer, more connected local economy

We want AYE Marketplace to become the default place Tigrayans go to buy, sell, and hire — known for being safe, easy, and genuinely useful, not just another app. Every feature we build starts from that promise.
