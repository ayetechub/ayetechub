🛍️ For Buyers

Find it, check it, contact the seller your way

AYE Market is built around one idea: you should never have to guess. Every listing carries real photos with a full-screen zoom gallery, a detailed description, the seller's verification status, and the exact location on a map — before you ever reach out.

  • Powerful search — keyword search across every category, with singular/plural matching so "phone" also finds "phones".
  • Advanced filters — narrow by price range, condition, brand, model, and category-specific attributes like bedrooms, mileage, or storage size.
  • Nearby search — with GPS permission, see listings sorted by real distance from where you stand.
  • Tigray location system — browse by the real administrative hierarchy: region, zone, wereda, tabiya, and city — not a generic address box.
  • Favorites & saved searches — save any listing to your Favorites, or save a whole search and get notified when new matches appear.
  • Four contact channels — in-app chat, phone call, WhatsApp, or SMS. The seller chooses which they accept; you choose which you prefer.

📤 For Sellers

Post in minutes, manage like a pro

Publishing a listing takes minutes: pick a category, add up to 8 photos, set your price and location, publish. But underneath, AYE Market gives sellers real tools — not just an ad slot.

  • Category-smart forms — every category has its own tailored fields, so your listing carries the details buyers filter by.
  • Full listing lifecycle — publish, edit, pause, mark sold, archive, duplicate, or delete from My Listings, anytime.
  • Auto-save drafts — your listing saves itself as you type; come back and finish whenever.
  • Seller Dashboard — track total views, favorites, sold counts, and (with Premium) deeper analytics and trends.
  • Reviews & reputation — buyers rate their experience, and your rating shows on every listing you post.
  • Contact preference — accept chat only, calls only, or both. Your phone number is never shown unless you allow it.

🌍 Why AYE Market

Built for Tigray, Not Adapted For It

People in Tigray were already buying, selling, and hiring every day — through word of mouth, Telegram groups, and noticeboards. What was missing was a platform actually designed for it: local languages, local places, local ways of communicating, and safety features that address real local risks.

AYE Market started deliberately narrow. Instead of a generic "all of Ethiopia" app, we built the details right for one region first: full Tigrinya and Amharic translation, a location system that knows your tabiya, contact options that match how people here actually talk, and human moderation with local context.

AYE Market is developed by AYE Tech Hub — the same Ethiopian technology studio behind the AYE TechEngine & TechPro engineering education platform and the Habesha Hearts dating app.

❓ Questions

Common Questions

Is AYE Market really free? ▼
Yes. Creating an account, browsing, searching, posting listings, chatting, applying to jobs, and posting job openings are all completely free, with no commission taken on anything you sell. The only paid features are optional visibility boosts — the 7-Day Promotion, Premium, and Premium+ plans.
Which languages does the app support? ▼
Tigrinya (ትግርኛ), Amharic (አማርኛ), and English — fully translated across every screen, form, and notification. Switch anytime in Settings → Preferences → Language.
Do I need to verify my ID or phone to use the app? ▼
No. You can browse, message, buy, sell, post jobs, and apply to jobs without any verification. Verification is optional — it adds a visible trust badge to your profile and unlocks CV access for employers. You can verify with a Fayda ID, Kebele Residence ID, Driver's License, Business License, or Passport.
How do I pay for Premium or a promotion? ▼
Choose your plan in the app, select Manual Payment, follow the instructions shown, and upload your payment receipt. Our team reviews and activates it — usually within one business day. You'll get a notification the moment it's approved. Automatic Telebirr and Chapa payments are in development.
Does the seller see my phone number when I chat? ▼
No. In-app chat never reveals your phone number. Call, WhatsApp, and SMS buttons only appear when a seller has chosen to share their own number — and you're never required to share yours.
What phones does AYE Market work on? ▼
Android 8.0 (Oreo) and above. An iPhone version is on the roadmap but not yet available — the App Store badge below will light up when it ships.
How does AYE Market handle scams? ▼
Three layers: prevention (verification badges, seller ratings, published safety guidance), detection (one-tap reporting on every listing and profile, reviewed by human moderators), and enforcement (listing removal, warnings, suspensions, and bans). Read the full guide in our Safety Center.
Can I delete my account and data? ▼
Yes, permanently. Visit our Account Deletion page, sign in to verify it's you, and submit your request. Once approved, your login, profile, listings, photos, CV, and personal data are permanently deleted. You can also request a copy of your data first via the Privacy Rights page.
Is AYE Market only for Tigray? ▼
Today, yes — deliberately. We're building the experience deeply for one region first: its languages, its places, its market. Expansion to more Ethiopian regions is planned once the core experience is proven.
I found a bug or have a suggestion — where do I send it? ▼
Use the in-app Feedback & Support tool (Settings → Feedback) — it creates a support ticket our team answers directly in the app. Or email ayetechub@gmail.com.
