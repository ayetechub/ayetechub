Privacy Policy

How Habesha Hearts collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: October 10, 2026 · Applies to the Habesha Hearts app (package: com.habeshahearts.app) and this website.

Habesha Hearts ("we," "us," or "our") is operated by AYE Tech Hub. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect from users of the Habesha Hearts mobile application and website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights available to you. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to operate the dating service:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and gender, collected at sign-up (including via Google or Facebook sign-in).
  • Profile information: profile photos, bio text, voice bio recordings, interests, and any other details you choose to add to your profile.
  • Location data: approximate or precise location, used to show you nearby matches (see "Location Data Usage" below).
  • Messages and communications: content of chat messages, likes, matches, and reports you send within the app.
  • Device and usage information: device model, operating system, app version, IP address, crash logs, and in-app activity.
  • Payment information: if you purchase Premium or Premium+, payment is processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your full card number.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and maintain your account and profile.
  • Show you relevant matches and suggestions based on preferences, location, and activity.
  • Enable messaging, matching, likes, and other core dating-app features.
  • Verify profiles, detect fraud, and enforce our Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
  • Process subscription payments (Premium / Premium+) and manage billing.
  • Send important notifications (matches, messages, safety alerts, account updates).
  • Improve app performance and develop new features through aggregated analytics.

Location Data Usage

Habesha Hearts uses your device's location to show you potential matches who are nearby and to display an approximate distance on profiles. You can grant precise or approximate location access through your device's permission settings. Location access is optional — you may still use core features without enabling location, though match suggestions may be less relevant. We do not display your exact GPS coordinates to other users; only an approximate distance or city is shown. You may revoke location permission at any time from your device settings.

To protect accounts and prevent fraud, when you allow location access we also record your location (with the accuracy your phone reports) and the country your connection comes from. These records are visible only to authorised Habesha Hearts staff, never to other users.

Profile Photos

Photos you upload are stored securely on Firebase Storage and are shown to other users as part of your public profile, in line with standard dating-app functionality. Do not upload photos of anyone other than yourself, or any content that violates our Community Guidelines (nudity, minors, violence, or impersonation). We may remove photos or suspend accounts that violate these guidelines. You can delete or replace your photos at any time from your profile settings.

Chat Messages

Messages you send through Habesha Hearts are stored on our servers (Firebase Firestore) to deliver the conversation to the recipient and to support features such as message history, reporting, and moderation. We do not sell the content of your private messages. Our moderation team may review reported conversations solely to investigate violations of our Community Guidelines, harassment, or safety reports. Blocked or deleted conversations are removed from active display but may be retained briefly for safety and legal-compliance purposes.

Profile Verification

If you choose to verify your profile, you take a live selfie inside the app; the app asks you to blink, smile or turn your head to confirm it is a live person. The selfie is stored privately and reviewed only by our verification team to confirm that you match your profile photos. It is never shown to other users. To prevent the same picture being used on several accounts, we compare a simple fingerprint of the image (not a face template) against other submissions. We do not create or store biometric templates. Your verification selfie is deleted when you delete your account.

Device Information

We automatically collect certain technical information when you use the app, including device model, operating system and version, unique device identifiers, mobile network information, app version, language settings, and crash/diagnostic logs. This information helps us secure accounts, fix bugs, and ensure compatibility across devices.

Analytics

We use analytics tools, including Firebase Analytics and Google Analytics, to understand how users interact with the app — for example, which features are used most, session length, and retention. Analytics data is generally aggregated and used to improve the product; it is not used to publicly identify individual users.

Third-Party Services

Habesha Hearts relies on the following third-party service providers to operate the app. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Storage (photos/voice bios), Cloud Messaging (push notifications), and Crashlytics.
  • Google Analytics / Firebase Analytics: in-app usage analytics.
  • Google AdMob: may display ads to free-tier users and uses device identifiers (e.g. advertising ID) for ad delivery and limited ad personalization, subject to your device's ad-tracking settings.
  • Payment processor: Google Play Billing processes Premium and Premium+ subscription payments.
  • Google / Facebook Sign-In: optional social login providers, used only with your explicit authorization.

Google User Data

If you choose Sign in with Google, Habesha Hearts requests only the basic Google account information covered by the email and profile permissions: your name, email address, profile picture and your Google account identifier. We do not request access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google data.

How we use it: only to create and secure your Habesha Hearts account, to sign you in, and to pre-fill your name and photo on your profile (which you can change). Your email address is used for account and security messages and is never shown to other users.

Sharing: we do not sell Google user data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with third parties, except with Google Firebase, which stores it on our behalf to provide sign-in.

Storage and deletion: this information is kept while your account exists and is deleted when you delete your account (in the app, or at ayetechub.com/habesha-hearts/delete-account). You can also remove Habesha Hearts' access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Habesha Hearts' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With the service providers listed above, strictly to operate the app (hosting, storage, analytics, payments, push notifications).
  • With other users, limited to what you choose to display on your public profile (photos, bio, voice bio, approximate distance, online status).
  • With law enforcement or regulators, when required by law, legal process, or to protect the safety of our users.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with continued protection under this policy.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), Firebase security rules restricting access to authorized users, and access controls limiting employee access to personal data. While we work hard to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

User Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can:

  • Edit your profile information directly within the app at any time.
  • Delete individual photos, your voice bio, or specific conversations from your account.
  • Permanently delete your account and associated data from Settings → Account → Delete Account, or by emailing us at myhabeshadate@gmail.com with the subject line "DELETE MY ACCOUNT".
  • You can also submit a deletion request directly from our website: Delete My Account.
  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Some information may be retained for a limited period where required for legal, safety, fraud-prevention, or accounting purposes (e.g. transaction records).

Children's Privacy (18+ Only)

Habesha Hearts is strictly intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. Account creation requires confirming you are at least 18 years of age. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will suspend the account and delete the associated data as soon as reasonably possible. If you believe a minor is using the app, please contact us immediately at myhabeshadate@gmail.com.
