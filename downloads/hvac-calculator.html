📚 Engineering Notes

Method: Simplified ASHRAE CLTD/CLF method for commercial buildings.

Solar gains: Calculated using orientation average Solar Heat Gain Coefficient (SHGC) × peak irradiance (900 W/m² for hot climates).

Ventilation load: Outdoor air enthalpy difference × ventilation airflow rate (psychrometric calculation).

Safety factors: 10% added to cooling, 20% to heating for duct losses and supply air temperature variation.

Standards: ASHRAE 62.1 (ventilation), ASHRAE 90.1 (energy efficiency), ASHRAE Handbook of Fundamentals.
